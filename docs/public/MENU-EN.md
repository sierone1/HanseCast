# Menu – Hanse Cast 1.0.6

- **Player:** appearance, size and positioning; additional font, icon, time and comfort settings.
- **Target & focus:** enable, hide Blizzard target/focus cast bars separately, three designs, native color picker and swatches, dimensions and font size. **Show remaining time:** Off, Inside right, Outside right. Preview and separate positioning buttons remain accessible.
- **Profiles:** up to twelve player profiles, copy, rename, import/export. Compact target/focus preferences are shared independently of player profiles.
- **Help:** controls and diagnostics. `/hansecast report` opens a copyable report.

Feedback appears below the tabs. The color picker supports Okay and Cancel; positioning supports save/cancel. Settings changes are locked during combat.
